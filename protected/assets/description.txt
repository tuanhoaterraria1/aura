AURA is an AI agent project on Ethereum, launched through Programmable's protected Uniswap v4 launch.

Fees per trade (ETH side): 0.3% Programmable platform fee + 1% creator fee (paid to the creator wallet). No LP fee. Normal all-in: 1.3% (plus Programmable's 0.3% router fee when trading through Programmable).

Launch protection (enforced by contract):
- Opening buy fee starts at 99% total and decays to the normal 1.3% over the first 30 seconds; the extra amount goes to the Programmable platform treasury. Sells pay normal fees throughout.
- For the first 300 seconds, each wallet can receive at most 2% of supply and each buyer can buy at most 2% cumulatively, developer included. Buys in this window go through the canonical Uniswap Universal Router.
- Opening valuation of at least $15,000 FDV, checked against the Chainlink ETH/USD feed.

Supply: 1,000,000,000 AURA, all seeded into permanently locked liquidity; no later mint. The creator's initial buy is about 0.66% of supply.

The AURA AI agent runs off-chain: it analyses on-chain activity, publishes its reasoning, and may buy back and burn AURA with creator-fee funds. It has no on-chain control over fees or contracts.
